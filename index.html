Why this studio exists

When I arrived in France more than four years ago, I had studied French and I knew my grammar. And still, almost everything surprised me: the paperwork, the unwritten rules, the words people actually use in the street.

What helped me was people who took the time to explain. Alexandre Dubois Studio is my way of passing that on: books and resources for the French you need on a Monday morning at the préfecture, not only in the classroom.
